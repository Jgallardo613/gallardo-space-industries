Founder

Jeffrey Gallardo

Jeffrey Gallardo founded Gallardo Space Industries with one mission: build the hardware that brings warfighters home. A Computer Science student at BYU-Idaho with a 4.0 GPA, Jeffrey has already submitted SENTINEL AI to the United States Space Force through the Front Door program.


With a background spanning software engineering, systems design, and defense technology outreach, Jeffrey leads every GSI project from concept to prototype. He is actively pursuing a Space Force commission as a 62E Developmental Engineer Officer and brings six years of management and supervisory experience to the company.


Growing up in Compton, California as a first generation college student, Jeffrey's drive to build technology that protects people is personal. GSI is the vehicle for that mission.

Founder

Jeffrey Gallardo

Jeffrey Gallardo grew up in Compton, California with one dream: to leave and do something meaningful. Space was always the destination. The stars were not just something to look at, they were something to reach for. That dream never left him.


Before college, Jeffrey served a two-year mission for the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. It changed how he sees the world. He learned to listen, to understand people's real needs, and to build solutions that actually matter. That mindset never left him either.


He started college, earned his Associates degree, and stepped away. Life had other plans. But Jeffrey came back, found his purpose in software and systems, and never looked back. Today he carries a 4.0 GPA in Computer Science at BYU-Idaho while simultaneously building a defense technology company from scratch, with little to no outside funding, no excuses, and no quit.


The military has always been family. From ROTC as a kid to actively pursuing a Space Force commission as a 62E Developmental Engineer Officer, service is part of who he is. SENTINEL AI, submitted to the United States Space Force through the Front Door program, is proof that one person with the right purpose can move fast and build things that matter. GSI exists to bring our people home.
